🥉 Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Chamber directory listing with contact details
  • Access to all networking events
  • Monthly business newsletter
  • Use of Chamber meeting rooms (4 hours/month)
  • 10% discount on Chamber training workshops
  • Business referral program access

🥈 Silver Membership Benefits

  • All Bronze benefits
  • Featured directory listing with logo and description
  • Spotlight advertising on Chamber home page (rotating)
  • Priority seating at Chamber events
  • 25% discount on training workshops and seminars

  • Social media mentions (2 per month)
  • Access to exclusive Silver/Gold member events

Fee: ₦150,000 / year

🥇 Gold Membership Benefits

  • All Silver benefits
  • Premium homepage spotlight (guaranteed monthly feature)
  • Logo on Chamber letterhead and publications
  • VIP access to all Chamber events with reserved tables
  • 50% discount on all workshops and training
  • Unlimited use of Chamber meeting rooms
  • Weekly social media features and promotion
  • Direct introductions to government and business leaders
  • Annual business profile in Chamber Annual Report

Fee: ₦400,000 / year
