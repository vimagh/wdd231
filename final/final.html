Website Site Plan

GatherFaith

A faith-based events discovery platform for Christian singles

Section 01

Site Name

GatherFaith

The name GatherFaith was chosen because it captures the two core purposes of the platform: bringing people together (gather) around a shared Christian faith (faith). The word "gather" evokes community, warmth, and intentional connection — themes central to both the Christian tradition and the lived experience of singles seeking meaningful relationships and community. The combined word is short, memorable, and immediately communicates the site's mission.

Suggested domain: gatherfaith.org

Section 02

Site Purpose

GatherFaith is a nationwide events discovery platform designed specifically for Christian singles. The site provides a searchable, map-enabled directory of faith-based events — including retreats, worship nights, singles conferences, social mixers, and community service days — hosted by churches and ministries across the country. Visitors can search by location, date, and event type; churches and organizers can submit their own listings; and registered users can save favorites, set preferences, and subscribe to a weekly regional newsletter. The site also features a community stories section, where users share how faith-based events helped them find genuine connection.

Section 03

Scenarios

The following questions represent the kinds of things a typical GatherFaith visitor — a Christian single seeking community or connection — would come to the site to answer:

  • "Are there any Christian singles retreats or conferences happening near me this month that I can register for?"
  • "I'm visiting Abuja next weekend — are there any worship nights or faith-based social events I can attend while I'm there?"
  • "I lead a singles ministry at my church. How can I get our upcoming events listed so other Christian singles in the area can find us?"

Section 04

Color Scheme

The color palette draws from nature and faith — deep forest green for trust and growth, warm gold for warmth and hope, and soft cream for readability and calm. Together they communicate a welcoming, grounded, and reverent identity.

Forest Green — #1B5E42 Primary color. Used for the header, nav background, buttons, and headings. Conveys faith, growth, and stability.
Warm Gold — #C49A3C Accent color. Used for highlights, section labels, links, and call-to-action accents. Conveys warmth and hope.
Pale Green — #E8F4EE Supporting color. Used for card backgrounds, highlighted quotes, and subtle section fills.
Cream — #FAF8F3 Page background. Provides a soft, warm, and readable surface for all content.
Ink — #1C1C1C Body text and headings on light backgrounds. High contrast for readability and accessibility.

Section 05

Typography

Two fonts are used throughout the site, each serving a distinct role. Together they balance warmth and readability with elegance and character.

Display Font — Cormorant Garamond

Find Your People. Find Your Faith.

Used for: page headings (h1, h2), hero text, and section titles. A refined serif that communicates tradition, warmth, and spiritual depth. Applied at sizes ranging from 1.5rem to 3rem.

Body Font — Nunito

GatherFaith helps Christian singles discover faith-based events across the country — retreats, worship nights, conferences, and community gatherings all in one place.

Used for: body text, navigation links, labels, captions, form fields, and UI elements. A rounded humanist sans-serif that is highly legible, friendly, and approachable. Applied at 14px–16px for body copy.
