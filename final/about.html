Our Mission

GatherFaith exists to help Christian singles find genuine community through faith-based events across the country.

Why GatherFaith?

Christian singles across the country share a common challenge: finding meaningful community beyond their local congregation. Faith-based events — retreats, conferences, worship nights, social mixers — happen regularly in every region, but they are scattered across church websites, social media pages, and word of mouth.

GatherFaith was built to solve that problem. We are a dedicated, trusted discovery platform where singles can find events that align with their values — and where churches and ministries can reach people who are actively seeking connection.

What We Stand For

Every decision we make is guided by these four commitments

🙏

Faith First

Every event on GatherFaith is rooted in the Christian faith. We exist to serve people who love God and want to live that out in community.

🤝

Genuine Connection

We believe the best relationships form in person, around shared values and experiences. We champion events that create space for that.

🗺️

Nationwide Reach

Whether you're in a major city or a small town, GatherFaith surfaces events in your region so no one is left without community.

🌿

Open & Welcoming

Our platform welcomes singles of all denominational backgrounds. If it honors Christ and builds community, it belongs on GatherFaith.
